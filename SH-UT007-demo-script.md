# SH-UT007 — Evidence-to-Answer Studio Demo Script

**Status:** Product page and showroom listing published to GitHub; this script is prepared, but an MP4 has not yet been rendered or uploaded.

## 45–60 second demo

1. **Opening (0–5s):** Show the product name, UT-007 identity, and QR code.
2. **Question (5–12s):** Enter: “Does this claim have enough evidence?”
3. **Claim (12–20s):** Enter a sample factual claim.
4. **Source/evidence (20–30s):** Leave source/evidence notes blank; choose “Factual claim”.
5. **Evidence gate (30–38s):** Click “Build transparent answer” and show the status “INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE” and the phrase “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
6. **Source-based case (38–48s):** Add a source URL and concise evidence notes; rebuild and show “SOURCE-BASED · NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED”.
7. **Close (48–60s):** Show the limitation notice: a source URL alone does not independently verify a claim.
